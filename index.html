About Me

Practical engineering for ambitious ideas.

A builder mindset shaped by mechanical engineering, modern web development, and startup execution.

I'm a full-stack developer, UI/UX-focused builder, and technical founder from Solapur, Maharashtra, India. Through Lade Stack, I focus on MVP-first execution, AI-powered products, and startup-grade outcomes.
